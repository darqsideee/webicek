About Us

NovaSector is a premium FiveM community focused on performance, immersion, and fairness. Our purple-on-black identity reflects our mix of elegance and power. We deliver fast updates, active moderation, and community-driven features.

Our Mission

Provide a stable, enjoyable roleplay environment where creativity thrives and rules are clear.

  • Optimized server with minimal desync

Server Rules

  1. Respect all players and staff. No harassment or hate speech.
  2. No cheating, exploiting, or unfair third-party tools.
  3. Follow staff directions. Use proper channels for disputes.
  1. Roleplay first: value life and avoid RDM/VDM.
  2. Keep character names and actions in-universe.
  3. Use voice/text channels appropriately; no metagaming.
  1. Staff decisions are transparent and logged.
  2. Appeals via Discord with evidence where possible.
  3. Escalate issues respectfully and privately.

Full rules are in Discord and may change. Check regularly.
